Some people have an absurd view of the economy

as a magic thing that just produces stuff.

他们认为商品和服务神奇地来自某处，如果有人比其他人拥有更多东西，那是因为他们从这个神奇的东西来源中拿走了更多。359

展开英文原文

They think goods and services magically come from somewhere, and if somebody has more stuff than somebody else, it’s because they took more from this magic source of stuff.359

现在，让我对那些傻瓜们说清楚。如果我们不制造东西，就没有东西。如果我们不种植粮食、加工粮食、运输粮食……就没有食物。医疗、补牙，一切的一切。如果我们不制造东西，就没有东西。

展开英文原文

Now, let me break it to the fools out there. If we don’t make stuff, there’s no stuff. If we don’t grow the food, process the food, and transport the food…there’s no food. Medical treatments, getting your teeth fixed, everything. There’s no stuff if we don’t make stuff.

有些人已经脱离了现实。那种认为政府只需给每个人寄支票，一切就会好起来的想法显然是不对的。你不能仅仅通过立法印钱来解决问题。如果你不制造东西，就没有东西。整个机器可能会陷入停顿。360

展开英文原文

Some people have become detached from reality. This notion that the government can just send checks out to everybody and everything will be fine is not true—obviously. You can’t just legislate money to solve things. If you don’t make stuff, there is no stuff. The whole machine could grind to a halt.360

技术进步并非必然。它不是某种抽象的概念。是人类创造技术。如果我们不去做，它就不会发生。361

展开英文原文

Technological progress is not inevitable. It’s not some kind of abstract concept. Humans make technology. If we don’t do it, it will not happen.361

总得有人去做真正的工作。

展开英文原文

Somebody has to do the real work.

金融和法律领域的人才配置过多，尤其是在美国。太多聪明人进入了金融和法律行业。这既是赞美，也是批评。

展开英文原文

There’s an overallocation of talent in finance and law, especially in the United States. Too many smart people go into finance and law. This is both a compliment and a criticism.

我们应该让更少的人从事法律和金融，让更多的人去制造东西。

展开英文原文

Manufacturing used to be highly valued in the United States. These days it hasn’t been as much, which I think is wrong. Making cars is an honest day’s living, that’s for sure. 工厂即产品

展开英文原文

THE FACTORY IS THE PRODUCT

我在打造特斯拉时最大的顿悟是，

真正重要的是制造机器的机器——工厂。364

展开英文原文

The biggest epiphany I had building Tesla is

what really matters is the machine that builds the

machines—the factory.364

为了加速实现可持续的未来，特斯拉必须尽快扩大生产规模。这就是为什么特斯拉的工程重心转向了设计制造机器的机器——将工厂本身转变为产品。

展开英文原文

To accelerate a sustainable future, Tesla had to scale up production volume as quickly as possible. That is why Tesla engineering transitioned to focus heavily on designing the machine that makes the machines—turning the factory itself into the product.

对汽车生产进行第一性原理的物理分析表明，在约两年的迭代周期内，通过第三版设计，有望实现五到十倍的改进。365

展开英文原文

A first-principles physics analysis of automotive production suggests that somewhere between a fivefold to tenfold improvement is achievable by version three on a roughly twoyear iteration cycle.365

特斯拉坚信制造。苹果和谷歌则不。这是理念上的差异。我们相信制造技术本身蕴含着巨大的创新潜力。事实上，我们认为制造业的创新潜力远超汽车设计——远远超过。这只是理念上的不同。也许我们错了。但我们信仰制造，而一家像我们这样高度重视制造的公司，将吸引制造业最优秀的人才。366

展开英文原文

Tesla believes strongly in making things. Apple and Google do not. It’s a philosophical difference. We believe that manufacturing technology is itself subject to a tremendous amount of innovation. In fact, we believe there’s more potential for innovation in manufacturing than in the design of a car—by a long shot. This is just a philosophical difference. Perhaps we are wrong. But we believe in manufacturing, and a company that values manufacturing as highly as we do will attract the best minds in manufacturing.366

我们的成败不取决于竞争，

而取决于我们能否以人们负担得起的价格

制造出高质量的产品。367

展开英文原文

Our success or failure will not be because of

competition. It will be our capability to make

a high-quality product at a price people can

afford.367

你拥有这座巨大的工厂，一个控制论的集合体，有成千上万的问题出错，你必须全部解决。要快。如果你解决问题的速度不够快，工厂就无法运转。一家大工厂每停产一分钟，就会烧掉巨额资金。368

展开英文原文

You’ve got this giant factory, a cybernetic collective with ten thousand things going wrong and you’ve got to solve them all. Fast. If you don’t solve problems fast enough, the factory doesn’t go. A big factory burns a huge amount of money every minute you aren’t making a product.368

ATTACK THE CONSTRAINT

设计火箭很简单。

展开英文原文

Designing a rocket is trivial.

有大量的书可以读，如果你能理解方程式，你就能设计火箭。真的很容易。但哪怕只是制造出其中一个并让它进入轨道，都极其困难。369

展开英文原文

There are tons of books you can read, and if you can understand equations, you can design a rocket. Real easy. But making even one of these things and getting it to orbit is very hard.369

汽车也是如此。制造一辆原型车很容易，但实现量产却很难。370

展开英文原文

It’s the same thing for cars. It’s easy to make a car prototype; it’s hard to do car production.370

这一点常被低估。人们以为存在某个“灵光一现”的时刻，想出点子就万事大吉。他们相信设计是难点，生产不过是复制而已。这完全错误。371

展开英文原文

This is underappreciated. People think there is a “eureka” moment where you come up with an idea and that’s it. They believe design is the hard part and production is just making copies. That’s completely false.371

在特斯拉，我们学到了宝贵的一课。整条生产线的速度，取决于其中最慢、最不走运的那个环节。假设生产要顺利进行，需要一万件事都做对。如果你有九千九百九十九件事都运转正常，只有一件出了问题，那它就决定了生产速度。372

展开英文原文

At Tesla, we learned a valuable lesson. The production line will move as fast as the slowest and least lucky part of the entire production line. Let’s say there are ten thousand things that have to go right for production to work. If you have 9,999 things working and one that isn’t, that sets the production rate.372

事情的进展速度，取决于最不走运或最不称职的供应商。你能想到的任何自然灾害，都曾发生在我们的供应商身上。有家供应商的工厂被烧毁。地震。海啸。特大冰雹。龙卷风。一艘船沉没。墨西哥边境发生枪战。我不是在开玩笑。这延误了后备箱地毯的供应。373

展开英文原文

Things move as fast as the least lucky or least competent supplier. Any natural disaster you care to name has happened to our suppliers. A supplier had a factory burn down. An earthquake. A tsunami. Massive hail. A tornado. A ship sank. A shoot-out at the Mexican border. I’m not kidding. That delayed trunk carpet.373

因此，在扩大SpaceX规模时，我们花在设计制造系统上的精力，是设计猛禽发动机的十倍到一百倍。374 我们先造火箭，后建工厂，因为构建生产系统才是更难的事。375

展开英文原文

So when scaling SpaceX, we spent ten to one hundred times more effort on designing the manufacturing system than on designing the Raptor engine.374 We built the rockets first and the factory later, because building the production system is the harder thing.375

设计被高估了，制造却被低估了。投入到生产系统中的工作量，是产品本身的一千倍，甚至一万倍。376 尤其是对于采用新技术的产品。制造的难度，与产品中新技术含量成正比。377

展开英文原文

Design is overrated, and manufacturing is underrated. There is 1,000 percent, maybe 10,000 percent more work that goes into the production system than the product itself.376 Especially for a product with new technology. The difficulty of manufacturing is proportionate to the amount of new technology in the product.377

MANUFACTURING IS THE MOAT

决定制造业竞争力的两个要素是：规模经济和技术。如果你将技术水平最大化，同时将规模最大化，这显然会形成最具竞争力的局面。这就是工厂如此庞大的原因。378

展开英文原文

Two things define manufacturing competitiveness: economies of scale and technology. If you maximize your level of technology and maximize your level of scale, this is obviously going to be the most competitive situation. That’s why plants are so freaking giant.378

我们在得克萨斯州的工厂，从运入原材料的火车车皮开始。我们制造电芯和电池包，组装电机，铸造零部件。我们还引入了一项重大创新，即将汽车整个前部和后部各作为一个整体铸件。这个想法来自玩具车。

Our plant in Texas starts from actual rail cars of raw materials coming in. We form the battery cell and the battery pack, build the motor, and cast the parts. We also have introduced a major innovation, which is to cast the entire front third and rear third of the car as a single piece. I got this idea from toy cars.
