DON’T WASTE TIME

过多的会议是大公司的顽疾，而且几乎总是随着时间推移愈演愈烈。取消所有大型会议，除非你确信它们能为全体与会者提供价值，即便如此，也要保持简短。

展开英文原文

Excessive meetings are the blight of big companies and almost always get worse over time. Get rid of all large meetings, unless you’re certain they are providing value to the whole audience, in which case, keep them short.

还要取消频繁的会议，除非你正在处理极其紧急的事务。一旦紧急事务解决，会议频率应迅速降低。

展开英文原文

Also get rid of frequent meetings, unless you are dealing with an extremely urgent matter. Meeting frequency should drop rapidly once the urgent matter is resolved.

一旦明显看出你无法再增加价值，就立即离开会议或挂断电话。离开并不失礼；失礼的是让别人留下并浪费他们的时间。

展开英文原文

Walk out of a meeting or drop off a call as soon as it is obvious you aren’t adding value. It is not rude to leave; it is rude to make someone stay and waste their time.

真正实现知识产权保护的方式是快速创新。如果你的创新速度很快，就不必担心保护知识产权，因为其他公司将会抄袭你多年前的成果。这没关系。只要确保你的创新速度够快就行。创新的速度才是关键。333

分解“不可能”

展开英文原文

BREAK DOWN THE “IMPOSSIBLE”

第一性原理的通用原则适用于软件、硬件，实际上适用于任何事物。我们经常被告知某事不可能，但一旦我们将其分解为基本要素，就能解决它们。344

展开英文原文

The general principles of first-principle thinking apply to software, hardware, anything really. Often, we were told something was impossible, but once we broke it down into its constituent elements, we could solve those.344

2024年，对于xAI，我们试图构建一个训练超级集群（一个用于训练AI模型的巨型计算机网络）。我们找到各个供应商，说我们需要十万个H100（超强计算机芯片）才能进行连贯训练。他们估计完成这项工作需要十八到二十四个月。然而，我们需要在六个月内完成，否则我们将失去竞争力。

展开英文原文

For xAI in 2024, we were trying to build a training supercluster (a giant network of computers to train an AI model). We went to the various suppliers and said we needed one hundred thousand H100s (superpowerful computer chips) to be able to train coherently. Their estimates for how long it would take to complete that were eighteen to twenty-four months. Well, we needed to get that done in six months or we wouldn’t be competitive.

于是，我们将其分解。我们需要一栋建筑；我们需要电力；我们需要冷却。

展开英文原文

So, we broke that down. We need a building; we need power; we need cooling.

我们没有足够的时间从零开始建造一栋建筑。因此，我们在孟菲斯找到了一家不再使用的工厂。输入功率是15兆瓦，而我们需要150兆瓦。于是，我们租用了发电机，并将它们安置在建筑的一侧。

展开英文原文

We didn’t have enough time to build a building from scratch. So, we found a factory in Memphis that was no longer in use. The input power was 15 megawatts and we needed 150 megawatts. So, we rented generators and put them along one side of the building.

接着，我们需要冷却。于是，我们租用了美国大约四分之一的移动冷却能力，并将冷却器放置在建筑的另一侧。

展开英文原文

Then, we needed cooling. So, we rented about a quarter of the mobile cooling capacity of the US and put the chillers on the other side of the building.

这并没有完全解决问题，因为训练期间的功率波动非常大。功率可能在100毫秒内下降50%，而发电机无法跟上。于是，我们添加了特斯拉Megapack，并修改了Megapack中的软件，以便在训练运行期间平滑功率波动。

展开英文原文

That didn’t fully solve the problem because the power variations during training are very big. Power can drop by 50 percent in 100 milliseconds, which the generators can’t keep up with. So then we added Tesla megapacks and modified the software in the megapacks to be able to smooth out the power variation during the training run.

然后还有一系列网络挑战。为十万个GPU（图形处理单元）铺设网络电缆极其困难。我们让网络团队分四班倒，全天候进行布线。我本人也睡在数据中心，并亲自参与布线。345

展开英文原文

Then there were a bunch of networking challenges. Networking cables for one hundred thousand GPUs (graphics processing units) are very, very difficult. We ran the networking team to do the cabling in four shifts, 24/7. I was sleeping in the data center and doing cabling myself.345

现在被称为Colossus，它是目前世界上最大的AI训练平台。它在122天内建成。92天后，它已翻倍至二十万个GPU。346

展开英文原文

Now called Colossus, it is currently the largest AI training platform in the world. It was constructed in 122 days. Ninetytwo days later, it had been doubled to two hundred thousand GPUs.346

对于内部时间表，我们设定尽可能激进的时间节点。我这样做是因为进度有一种“气体膨胀定律”。无论你设定多长时间，实际用时都不会少于它。事情很少能比计划更快完成。349

For internal timelines, we set the most aggressive timelines we can. I do this because there’s a kind of “law of gaseous expansion” for schedules. Whatever time you set, it’s not going to be less than that. It’s rare that something will ever get done faster than the schedule.349

当我提出一个时间表时，那确实是我认为真实可行的时间表。它不是我编造的虚假时间表。它可能有些妄想——这完全有可能，而且这种情况也时有发生。但它从来都不是一个明知虚假的截止日期，从来没有。350
